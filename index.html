System Overview

ForgeIQ automates the quotation, procurement, and lifecycle tracking of precision manufacturing parts. Instead of relying on error-prone vision models for critical engineering dimensions, ForgeIQ utilizes deterministic CAD geometry extraction (ezdxf) to parse 2D DXF drawings into piercing counts, cutting tool travel perimeters, and raw sheet utilization bounds. It couples this with RAG-driven retrieval over customer notes, a 9-stage real-time production tracking pipeline, and an integrated buyer portal.
